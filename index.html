HISTORICAL RECORD CSE INDUCTION

The formal transition of incoming engineers into the Department of Computer Science.

Staged within the acoustic geometry of Kalam Auditorium, Lumière welcomed the newly inducted batch into departmental life. Moving far past standard collegiate ceremonies, the hall was transformed through a deliberate triad of crimson drapery, molten gold accents, and pitch-black structural framing.

Custom illuminated panels traced the hall's perimeter, setting a razor-sharp backdrop for technical project demonstrations, student artistic showcases, and the official pledge of engineering rigor.
