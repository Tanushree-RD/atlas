Lumière – The Gala

The landmark branch entry celebration welcoming students into the Department of Computer Science & Engineering.

29 October 2025 Kalam Auditorium
OFFICIAL THEME: “Where Glam Meets Glow”

Held in the grandeur of Kalam Auditorium, Lumière marked the auspicious transition of emerging engineering minds into the CSE department. Enveloped in an opulent palette of crimson red, radiant gold, and deep black decor, the auditorium came alive with illuminated neon stage panels, electrifying student showcases, and an induction honoring academic camaraderie.
